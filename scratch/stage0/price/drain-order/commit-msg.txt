A drain's run lands durable at the writer's ask after its presync, priced level, and the presync's seams cost one load

What a caller saw: nothing. The order this holds was landed by an earlier
commit without a price: a closing run whose records the writer presyncs
(`SealJob::awaits_writer`) is named durable only at the writer's Drain
ask, after the presync has returned, so a failed presync stops the seal
queue before any manifest names the run's segment. That serialises the
durable landing behind the presync and adds a hop to the writer's ask,
where the shape before landed the job whenever the seal thread returned.

Priced against that shape (kept in the measuring tree only, as
`Options::price_drain_order_old` with the arms `supdb-drainorderold` and
`supdb-ingestdrainorderold`), it costs nothing measurable: the drain's
stamps put the presync's return and the ask before the seal thread's
return in 207 of 208 drains, so both shapes land from the ask and the
landing waits for the seal thread as it did. `strace -f -c` over
`bench/examples/load_only.rs` read the same file system calls in both
arms. Pairings (B/A, new against old; no cell marked in either sitting):

  sh bench/scripts/pair.sh supdb supdb-drainorderold {10000,300000} --passes load
    sitting 1: 10k ordered 1.02, shuffled 0.98; 300k ordered 1.02, shuffled 1.02
    sitting 2: 10k ordered 0.97, shuffled 1.03; 300k ordered 0.97, shuffled 0.96
  sh bench/scripts/pair.sh supdb-ingest supdb-ingestdrainorderold {10000,300000} --passes load
    sitting 1: 10k ordered 0.97, shuffled 0.98; 300k ordered 1.01, shuffled 0.96
    sitting 2: 10k ordered 1.00, shuffled 0.97; 300k ordered 1.02, shuffled 0.99

With SUPDB_LOAD_PHASES=1 SUPDB_DRAIN_STEPS=1 before the same arms, the
drain's gap from the later of the fsync and the presync to the join read
level: durable 10k 76/75 and 91/68 us, 300k 145/176 and 157/152 (a rerun
139/134 and 117/181); buffered 10k 79/64 and 65/67, 300k 176/172 and
176/186 (new/old, per sitting). Device bytes were identical.

The presync's two test seams, a hold the writer's presync loaded and a
failure flag it swapped on every presync -- an atomic read-modify-write
on the success path -- are one byte now (`Shared::presync_seam`:
`PRESYNC_FAIL`, `PRESYNC_HOLD`, and `PRESYNC_WAITING`, which a held
presync sets while it waits), loaded once with Acquire; a nonzero byte
goes to a cold `seamed_presync`. A presync no test armed does one load
where it did a load and a swap (read off the source, not counted). A
debug assertion in `hand_seal` holds that a job awaiting the writer is
handed only during a drain, whose ask lands it; and a flush cannot send
no ask after such a hand-over, since `close_direct` waits for room before
it takes the run and nothing after the take can fail, which a comment in
`Db::flush` says. CLAUDE.md's presync entry says how the order was priced.

Tests (tests/durable.rs, section drain-order): a drain names its run's
segment durable before it answers, on the background and the inline
segment work and under Encryption::Cipher, where a run's commits are log
frames and there is no writer presync to fail; and the seams held apart
(a hold and a failure, a failure armed before and under a hold taken at
the lift, the inline arm's presync not held, an injected failure taken
once). The drain tests pass on the commit before as well, since no
behaviour changed, and are not evidence of a fix. Held to 40 mutants
over four rounds; three survive: the hand_seal assertion deleted (it
holds by construction, and a caller that hands outside a drain fires
it), the landing gate restated as `awaits_writer && draining`
(equivalent under that assertion), and the wedge moved after the
writer's own record of the failure (two consecutive stores on the
writer's thread, the second read by the writer alone).

Left open: the durable 300k pairing's readable-landing stamp read longer
in the new arm in every sitting, before every instruction the arms
differ in, and no pairing of an arm against its own twin was run to
read it as position; Encryption::Kernel was not run; the presync path
was not counted under callgrind.
