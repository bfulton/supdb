A store's residency and advice are asked of its byte source by offset, one span question answered for a mapped file and a paged mapping alike

What is held. Bytes::resident_share(off, len) is the one span residency
question, answered by MmapBytes for both kinds: a mapped file by mincore
over its pages by offset, a paged mapping by Paging::resident. The three
answers it replaces are deleted with every caller moved
(MmapBytes::paged_resident, MmapBytes::resident_pages, db::resident_share),
and so are OrdIndex::mapped and Blob::record_region_span. The plain build
places its asks and drops by offset (Blob::record_region_place and the new
crate-private record_dir_place) through the mapping's own advice;
warm_file measures the mapping's length and asks through advise_willneed;
raw_read_span answers from the header for both kinds of reader; the three
sites that advise a file by its descriptor ask Seg::records_by_descriptor.
No residency or advice call outside the byte-source seam takes an address
now, but the CPU-cache prefetches, which give no answer and are left by
name for the sealing unit.

How it was shown red. Before the change: a paged mapping and a mapped file
of the same bytes disagreed on what was resident (the pager's test read
0 pages against 256); an ordered index's heads read as not resident over a
source that holds them; a warm under a cipher measured the form file as
empty and asked for nothing. Each is green after. Tests added since are
red against mutants of the lines they hold: a drop behind's window read
off the mapping's page table, the records' residency at a landing held
apart from the directory's, a file three quarters resident still warmed,
the envelope resident after a warm under a cipher.

A store in the clear. The cost count against the commit the unit began
at, over the read, scan and compact-form probes, the merge and the
landing, is green by function: no toggled probe moved a function, and the
moves in the untoggled probes are inside their bounds. Its syscalls are
held by a trace of seven tests of the compact form's binary, the crossing,
the build's drop behind, a dropped file asked back, the warm, the ordered
index's second drop, a merge's drops and the stride switch, each run three
times at the base and at the tree: every call shape the tree makes the
base makes, and none is gone. A gate over the build-drop test now exits
red on a call shape new or missing against the base's runs; its control
over the tree's binary is green, and two mutants of the advice's offsets
are red under it.

What changes under a cipher, on paths no default reaches there (the
compact form under ZPages::Always, or merge_read_ahead): the warm asks for
the envelope's cold pages and decrypts nothing; a form file's and an
ordered index's residency are asked of the pager instead of decrypting the
whole file; the heads' residency answers by the pager where it always
answered no; a hot-raw release asks for the raw records' ciphertext; a
merge's read-ahead asks its inputs' ciphertext. These are priced with the
compact form under a cipher, at the stage that prices it, and a pairing
with merge_read_ahead set is owed by whoever turns it on.

Mutants. The plan's four and the unit's own over three rounds, each
recorded with what killed it. A page-aligned start of the build's ask is
seen by no test, the kernel reading the same pages either way, and is held
by the trace gate. Three survivors are equivalent on every reachable
state: a records' residency default that only a sparse reader or a failed
mincore could reach, a form file asked open by path in place of the
door, and an offset past usize.

Left open. The pager keeps no count of the advice it is given (bytes
asked, drops, residency asks), so what the crossing and the build ask of
it under a cipher is held by outcomes alone; the count is owed to the page
layer's stage. The warm's touch goes through Bytes::lend_at, which the
sealing unit closes to the format's readers and has to give the warm
another way. The stride form's plain build is not under the trace, no test
of that binary building one; its offsets are held against offsets taken by
pointer subtraction. Not built for macOS or aarch64 here. No timing was
taken.
