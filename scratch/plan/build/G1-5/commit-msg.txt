A key section read by the piece has its checksum row verified at open unless its source says it authenticates what it lends

`Bytes::authenticated` says whether a source has checked every byte it
lends before lending it; it defaults to false. A paged mapping answers
true, since its pager lends no chunk whose tag it has not checked, and a
mapped file answers false. `Blob::open_with` used to skip the key
section's checksum row for any section held by the piece. It now sends
such a section to `verify_row_by_piece` (cold, out of line), which skips
the row only over an authenticated source and otherwise verifies it
through `flatindex::verify_pieces_in`: the row asked for once, each piece
asked for on its own, never the section whole. The arm for a section
held whole is the code it was.

How it was shown red: the new test in tests/blob.rs flips a byte of the
key section of a segment opened over a source that lends by the piece
and checks nothing, and requires the refusal the whole reader gives, in
its words. Before the change the flip opened ("a flip at section offset
18471 opened over a source by the piece that checks nothing"). The test
now flips the last byte of every piece, the short first piece and the
piece ending at the row among them, and holds the open of an unchecked
piece source to asking every byte with no ask longer than a piece. A
second test forges a piece whose CRC moves in its top byte alone. The
test that a source by the piece answers the same opens an authenticated
source now, so its bound of under a page asked at the open is that
source's bound.

What it cannot see: no source the engine makes takes the new path (every
piece source the engine makes is a paged mapping, which authenticates),
so the verify by the piece runs only for an outside `Bytes` that lends by
the piece and does not say it authenticates. That no third party
implements `Paging` is not checked; it is doc-hidden public, and only
the crate's `MmapBytes::paged` builds one.

What a store in the clear pays: counted with callgrind by function
against the commit this began at, over the load, commit, seal, scan_at,
merge and land probes, cost: green. `Blob::open_with` reads three
instructions more for each segment opened in the loads and the same in
the merge and the landing, where some of its instructions moved between
inlined lines with the same total. Nothing is added a key, an entry, a
block or a step, no syscall is added, and the new functions run in no
probe.

Under a cipher: nothing changes in behaviour. The store's sources answer
authenticated, so its open still skips the row, and the cipher store's
open stays within its bound of chunks (tests/cipher.rs), which is what
fails when a paged mapping answers false. Nothing new starts to work
there and no pricing is owed.

Mutants: the unit's list and others on the new lines, among them the
default answering true, the skip keyed on the piece kind alone, the
verify asking for the section whole, a mapped file or an empty mapped
file answering true, a paged mapping answering false, the verdict
dropped, a mismatch passed, three bytes of the word compared, the base
aligned to the piece, and the first, the last, a short or a middle piece
asked for and never compared. Each is killed; the first-, last- and
short-piece mutants survived the test before it flipped a byte in every
piece, and the empty-file mutant survived until a test mapped an empty
file.

Left open: `BlobOptions::read_ahead` is not given to the walk by the
piece, since no library source reaches it and the hook advises a
mapping. `crypt::Encrypted` keeps the default false; it never lends by
the piece, so true would change nothing.
