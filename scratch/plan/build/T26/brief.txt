HOW TO WAIT. An agent never ends its turn to wait for anything: a turn that ends is taken as the step's answer, at once, with whatever is unfinished reported as unfinished. A command that outlasts one call is started in the background with its output and its exit status in a log ("...; echo EXIT $? >> log") and waited for in a foreground call: `until grep -q '^EXIT' <log>; do sleep 20; done; tail -5 <log>` with the call's timeout set to 600000, issued again each time it times out. A call that begins with a bare `sleep` is refused; the Monitor tool and a background wait both return at once, so neither is the way to wait, whatever another instruction of this launch says about Monitor. Before answering, kill what you started that still runs, put the tree back from your newest kept copy by sha256 if a mutant or a scratch copy is in it, and say in the notes what remains.
